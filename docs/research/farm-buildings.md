## Summary
1. **The factory's recipe scales:** stone-brick/cobble plinth ramp, close-studded or board-clad timber frame, layered roof (eave course, ridge cap, dormers/gablets), one chimney at most, 2-3 volumes, whole-plot scene. Each farm place keeps the *recipe* but gets its own **roof form, wall skin and silhouette** so the five do not look like five factories: Mill = tall stone-and-timber with a big wheel; Cow Barn = long aisled barn with half-hips, catslide and a cart porch; Sugar Works = terraced brick works with a round horse-mill; Cocoa Grove = raised jungle-timber lodge and open drying sheds; Chicken Farm = coop on staddle stones plus a square dovecote.
2. **What separates paid/top-rated builds from ours is not block count, it is the setting:** custom cliff/waterfall and trees, big farmland plots, winding paths, carts (BiggyDiggy7 windmill, BuiltByBit); functional-looking interiors visible through openings (Coniferous watermill, PMC); darker, simpler palettes with strong sculpted dirt (CubicinCubic watermill, GrabCraft). So the generator needs a **terrain/scene layer** per place at least as strong as the building.
3. **Scale:** our factory is 19x18x14 (965 blocks). Reference barns are 1.2k-3k blocks, windmills 0.5k-1.7k, watermills 1.7k, all 12-25 wide. Target **1,500-3,500 blocks per hero building** (mill, barn, works), 600-1,200 for the secondary ones; cap 40,000 per file is no limit.
4. **New kits needed** (section 8): waterwheel pit + race, sails/hub, round-and-octagon solver (horse-gin, dovecote, tower), half-hip and catslide roof solver, hay-roll thatch roof, barn double door with braces, hoist gablet (lucam) and beam, staddle stones, nest-box row, hurdle fence, drying rack, furrow fields, orchard grid, terrace/cut-and-fill. Plus 8 lint rules (section 9).
5. Four decisions for the user (section 11).

### Plantations, orchards, sugar
- No premium or top-rated *cocoa plantation* or *sugar press works* showcase turned up on PMC/BBB (searches for both returned only farming-mechanic pages and mods). Treat Cocoa Grove and Sugar Works as **designed from real-world facts**, `[D]`.
- Orchard layouts: regular grid, quincunx, hexagonal, contour/terrace planting; grazed or mown grass beneath (Wikipedia Orchard) **[read]**.
- Sugar: pre-industrial mill complex = mill (wind, water or cattle), boiling house, still house, **laid out down a slope so juice flows downhill**; cattle mill = round covered building, hardwood posts or masonry pillars, shingled roof, oxen on long levers; 3 vertical rollers driven through a cog (Wikipedia Sugar mill) **[read]**. Oast-style round kiln with conical roof and cowl, heat rising through perforated floors, is the model for a **drying/curing loft** (Wikipedia Oast house) **[read]**, `[D]` for sugar.
- Cocoa: pods grow on the trunk (build-quality section 3); fermentation in heaps/boxes then **sun-drying on trays/platforms with a rolling or removable roof** is standard practice (my knowledge; the Wikipedia page I tried 404'd, so `[D]`, verify if the user cares).

## 2. Shared rules for the five places (factory-like vs different)
Factory-like in every building (keep): stone/cobble/brick **plinth ramp** (3 courses, cracked/mossy clusters), timber frame **in dark oak/spruce** with braces, **eave and ridge course in a darker block**, 1-block eaves, at least one **bracketed or stepped** feature (jetty, hood, corbel), **at most one chimney**, 45-degree primary pitch, **lit windows** (lantern behind), computed stair/pane states, a front yard scene.

Different per place (the new part): 
| Place | Roof form | Wall skin | Silhouette |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mill | gable + **half-hip** on the wheel side, gablet hoist | stone ground floor, plaster+frame upper | tall (ridge ~13), wheel disc on the long side |
| Cow Barn | long **gable with half-hipped ends + catslide outshut** | **vertical board with battens** on frame, brick/cobble plinth | long and low, huge door porch |
| Sugar Works | **hipped** boiling house + **conical** horse-mill roof + lean-to loft | brick with stone quoins | terraced downhill, three roof forms |
| Cocoa Grove | **hay-roll thatch hip** (lodge), open lean-to sheds | jungle planks, orange terracotta, on stone stilts | raised, open, vertical jungle trunks |
| Chicken Farm | **catslide** coop, **pyramid/louvre** dovecote | plaster + frame, wattle fence | tiny stilted coop + 1 tall tower |

Eye-level readability (player at ~10-15 blocks, FOV 70; build-quality viewpoint math applies) `[D]`:
- A 1-block detail disappears at 15 blocks unless it contrasts in value with its neighbour (dark frame on light plaster reads, plaster on plaster does not). Make the main read **value contrast of frame vs infill and roof vs walls**.
- Details that read: door and hood, big barn doors with braces, wheel disc, sails, hoist gablet, dormers, chimney, fence runs, staddle legs. Details that do not: single trapdoor shutters, 1-block flower pots, button vents. Put those only at the door/yard zone within 6 blocks.
- Keep the silhouette: ridge line broken twice (half-hip, gablet, cupola), eaves overhang 1-2, base spreads out.
- Vertical split: roof about 40-45% of the visible height, walls 40%, plinth 15%.

## 5. Sugar Works (sugar): terraced brick works with a horse-mill

## 6. Cocoa Grove (chocolate): jungle plantation, medieval details
Footprint of the lodge **11 x 9** (ridge y 9) on a 1-block stone plinth with **pier posts** at the front; **fermentation shed 7 x 5** (open on one side); **drying platform 9 x 5**. About 1,600 blocks + grove (trees count separately). The only place allowed a real jungle vibe (user decision 6), still medieval: walled and terraced, shared dark-oak door family.
- **Lodge:** `mossy_stone_bricks` plinth, `jungle_planks` / `stripped_jungle_log` / `orange_terracotta` upper (orange/ochre read), `jungle_log` frame, **hay-roll hip roof**: body `jungle_stairs` at 45 degrees with a `hay_block` eave roll and ridge (the thatch kit), 2 dormers; veranda in front on posts with a `spruce_trapdoor` lattice and hanging `lantern`; **no chimney** (cooking is on an open `campfire` under a smoke louvre).
- **Fermentation shed (open timber):** 4 `jungle_log` posts + beams, `jungle_slab` shed roof, **sweating boxes**: rows of `barrel`s/`composter`s stepped down a slope (each lower than the last), banana-leaf cover = `green_carpet`/`moss_carpet`.

## 10. Lint rules to add (`design/build-lint.md`; each is a measurable check)
- **L-F1 volumes:** hero buildings have at least 2 roof volumes (distinct ridge/axis or height) each >=20% of the footprint.
- **L-F2 roof diversity:** a building uses at least two roof forms of {gable, half-hip, hip, catslide, cross-gable, cone, pyramid}; no roof plane larger than 7 x 7 blocks without a break (dormer, gablet, ridge step), build-quality [S5].
- **L-F3 yard:** each place keeps a free rectangle (e.g. 11 x 7) in front of the main entrance and within 5 blocks of the viewpoint with no block above ground (except fence-height props at the edges); hologram, panel and ripe-spot cells are inside/adjacent.
- **L-F4 clearance:** wheel disc and sails volumes (radius + margin) intersect no block; the race water is enclosed; every cocoa pod has its jungle log behind it.
- **L-F5 door sanity:** a double barn door's opening >= 3 wide has a lintel one block wider each side; no 2-block-high cavities for livestock doors.
- **L-F6 ground:** no 5 x 5 patch of a single ground block outside fields and water; edge noise >=1 on paths/ponds; field beds have both crop and path rows.
- **L-F7 chimney/vent:** at most one chimney per building (R11), `vent`/`louvre` elements are tagged and exempt; a campfire with no chimney must sit under a louvre or open roof.
- **L-F8 value contrast:** frame vs infill and roof vs wall differ by >=1 ramp step (use a lightness table of the palettes); stairs make up >=15% of the blocks of any roofed building (the GrabCraft data: 25%+ for the best-looking ones, `[D]` threshold).
