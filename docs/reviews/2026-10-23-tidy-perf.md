# Tidy: performance measurements

Plugin-tester (Gandalf) · 2026-10-23 · base `8940d31` · JDK temurin 25.0.4 · 24 cores · Linux

## How to run
`mise run test` runs `src/test/java/dev/casperrs/cookieClicker/view/TidyPerfTest.java` with the rest of the suite (3 tests,
about 4 s). It prints `[tidy-perf] ...` lines and writes `target/tidy-perf.txt`. No JMH: 3000 warm-up calls, then 7 rounds of
4000 calls, **median** ns per call. It asserts nothing about time, so a slow machine cannot fail the build. To see just
these numbers: `MAVEN_ARGS="-Dtest=TidyPerfTest" mise run test`.

Numbers move about +-30 % between runs and machines (JIT, other load); compare **orders of magnitude and ratios**, and run
before and after on the same machine in the same sitting. The fixture is a mid-game player (8 of 11 buildings at 40 owned, 6
one-time upgrades bought, 5e9 cookies in hand) with the shipped `buildings.yml`, `upgrades.yml`, `messages.yml`, `config.yml`.

### What the numbers mean (estimates, not measured on a server)
- Idle viewer standing at their bakery: 1 Hz path = produce (0.6 us) + HUD (1.2 us) + rows (7.4 us, twice because of the duplicate
  refresh) = about 16 us per second. Negligible; the dedupe (PERF-U2) saves half of the rows part.
- Viewer who clicks (15/s): per click 0.15 (click) + 1.2 (HUD) + 7.4 (rows) + 2.6 (text parse) = about 11 us, ~170 us/s per
  clicking player. 100 clickers = 17 ms/s, under 2 % of one core. Acceptable; PERF-U9 and PERF-V1 trim it.
- Viewer with rolling prices (Max mode, or right after buying): rows re-render ~4 times per second, 8-12 rows each, so about
  0.4 ms/s per viewer. **100 viewers = 40 ms/s = ~4 % of one core, ~0.8 ms of every 50 ms tick** on average, bursty (the
  Max-mode viewers roll together on the same second tick). This is the one path that scales with viewers and dominates the
  plugin's own tick share -> PERF-U1 (MiniMessage template compile) is the highest-value fix.

### What to profile with spark (in-game test guide for the PR)
`/spark profiler start --timeout 60`, then for 60 s: 5+ players in their plots, two of them clicking the cookie and switching
the buildings panel to Max, one buying repeatedly. Check the plugin's share (search `CookieClicker` in the profile view):
- Good: total plugin share under ~2 % of the tick with 5 players; no `MiniMessage.deserialize` under `PanelUpdater.render`
  (after PERF-U1); `PlotTracker.update`/`WorldGuardHook.at` not visible unless players stand in the plot region (after PERF-W1).
- Bad: `PanelUpdater.render` or `buildingRow` above 0.5 % of the tick, `StationViews.refresh` above 0.3 %, `JdbcPlayerStore.save`
  stalling the IO thread at the autosave (IO thread, so it shows as `CookieClicker-IO`, not on the main thread).
