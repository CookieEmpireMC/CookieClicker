## Combined checks
- **Shipped files parse and validate.** `HouseDesignPointsTest.theShippedFactoryAndCottageParseWhenPresent` ran and was not skipped (10 tests, 0 skipped in the surefire report).
- **Layout points.** The real `factory.yml` has `plot-size [31,30]`, house 19x14 at `[6,0,10]`, and entrance `[15,0]` on the north edge. The belt runs `[18,0,11]` to `[18,0,7]`, which is 5 cells and meets the "straight, at least 4" criterion. The pedestal `[18,0,6]` and shop `[20,0,6]` sit just past the end of the belt. `belt_out [12,0,1]` is in design coordinates, so it lands on the south face of the house. That fits the design (the house front faces north).
- **Smoke and steam vs. loop cap.** The factory has 2 smoke and 2 steam points. That is 4 `Effects.loop`s per player, under `max-loops-per-player: 8`. This holds for the cottage too (1 loop). Headroom is 4. If a design ever has more than 8 points, the extra loops are not started; the effects engine logs this once.
- **`factory_steam` timing.** The loop is 37 ticks (prime) and the vents start staggered by `house.steam.every: 7`. About 4.3 particles/s per vent, with `force: true`.
- **`factory_ambient` via SoundService.** `ChimneySmoke` replays it every `house.ambient.every: 80` ticks, which equals the 4.0 s loop. `sounds.yml` has `pitch-min = pitch-max = 1.0`, volume 0.3, source `ambient`, and the fallback is the furnace crackle. Players within 24 blocks of the first steam point (or the house centre) hear it. It uses one per-player repeating task, which is cancelled with the other loops.
- **Cinematic timing (real 796-block factory, new defaults).** 796 non-air blocks over 17 layers at `blocks-per-tick: 2.25` and `layer-delay-ticks: 5`: 796/2.25 = 354 ticks, plus 18 x 5 = 90 ticks of layer pauses. That is about 22.2 s plus the fall time of the last blocks, so about 23-25 s. This is inside the 20-30 s target. The cottage (170 blocks) is over in a few seconds at this speed. The config comment says so and recommends 1 for the cottage.
- **Performance.** `PlotSetup` writes about 1,500 blocks in one main-thread step, without physics. Backups are written before that on the IO thread. This is an admin-only command and runs rarely. Fine (PERF-LOW at most). The cinematic stays at 2.25 blocks/tick with at most 24 falling displays per player. Nothing new runs per tick for every player.
