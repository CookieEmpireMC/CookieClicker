# Reference sheet: `cow_barn` (threshing barn with one corbie gable)

Sources: `docs/research/farm-buildings.md` sections 1 and 4 (Etoile barn seen by Rick 2026-10-25: roof about 60% of the facade, one stepped gable over a wide cart entrance with a fence gate, 2-3 small dormers; GrabCraft medieval barns: stairs are the largest block, 3-step plinth); Wikipedia Barn. Original design, nothing traced.

| What | Fact used | Where it shows |
|---|---|---|
| Massing | roof 55-60% of the facade, eaves y5, ridge y12, half-hipped ends, catslide outshut at the back | `hipped_roof` k 3 both ends + `catslide_roof` |
| Entrance | one strong stepped gable over a wide entrance with braced doors and a fence gate | `corbie_gable` + `barn_door_double` + spruce fence gates |
| Rhythm | 2-3 small gabled dormers breaking the long roof; hoist with a hay bale | dormers at x 12, 27 (hoist), 31 |

Ramps (light to dark): plinth cobblestone / mossy / andesite; board spruce / oak / dark-oak planks (stripped spruce log battens); masonry stone bricks / cracked / mossy; roof brick / mud brick / resin brick stairs (eave and rake dark oak, ridge stone brick); plaster (dairy) calcite / diorite / polished diorite. Greyscale: roof mid-dark red, boards mid, frame darkest, gables light.
