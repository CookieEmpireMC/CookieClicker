# Reference sheet: `mill` (stone watermill on its pond)

Sources: `docs/research/farm-buildings.md` sections 1 and 3 (first-hand looks by Rick on 2026-10-25 at the Coniferous half-timbered water mill, OrchiBuilds "Windspire Mill", Templario1408 "Silver Creek"; GrabCraft watermill listings; Wikipedia: Watermill, Granary, Staddle stone). Original design; nothing was traced or copied. The factory (`design/refs-factory.md`) is the craft model, not the cottage.

## Three references (technique, not copied)
| What | Fact used | Where it shows |
|---|---|---|
| Massing | the mill stands on a tall stone retaining wall rising out of the pond; the wheel sits in a stone slot between two piers under the overhang; water is about a third of the view | hall on its pond, wheel slot x 25..31 between piers, jettied upper floor, pond 20 x 11 in front |
| Roof | steep roofs (50-60 degrees), taller than the walls, at three or more heights, pent skirts and gablets; dark tiles against warm timber | 56 degree dark-oak shingle, deepslate-tile eave and rake, half-hip east end, wing, granary pyramid, dormers and hoist gablet |
| Detail | granary on staddle stones with a pyramid roof and outside steps; a sack hoist (beam, chain) on a gablet; a stone bridge over the race | granary at the yard, hoist gablet over the porch, fenced stone bridge over the head race |

## Ramps (light to dark, base first)
| Role | Blocks | Placement |
|---|---|---|
| plinth | cobblestone, mossy cobblestone, andesite | y <= 0 incl. the submerged retaining walls (moss at the waterline) |
| stone | stone bricks, cracked, mossy, tuff bricks | ground storey y1..4, pilasters, piers |
| plaster | calcite, diorite, polished diorite | upper floor, gables |
| timber | dark oak log, stripped dark oak log, dark oak planks | frame, bressumer, plate, risers |
| roof | dark oak stairs (field), spruce stairs (patches), deepslate tile stairs (eave, rake) | 56 degrees, ridge stripped dark oak + deepslate tile slab |
| floor | spruce, oak, dark oak planks | interior floors, granary boards |
| accents | oak planks (dormer cheeks), spruce trapdoors (shutters), waxed nothing | warm accent against the dark roof |

## Silhouette
Hall ridge y15 (cap y16) is the tallest; the east half-hip steps down; the wing (ridge y10) and the shed (y3..5) below it; granary pyramid y9/10 on the left of the yard; hoist beam and dormers break the north slope. Greyscale test: roof darkest, plaster lightest, stone mid.
