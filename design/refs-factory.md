# Reference sheet: `factory` (Norman/Tudor bakehouse)

(Brief contract said `design/refs/factory.md`; `.claude/ownership.json` only lets the model-designer write `design/*.md`,
so the sheets live at `design/refs-<house>.md`. Move them once `design/refs/**` is added to the globs.)

Style: English/Tudor half-timber on a Norman stone ground floor, a jettied plastered upper floor, a lean-to oven house with
the single brick stack, an open flour shed. Sources: `docs/research/build-quality.md` sections 2, 3, 7 (WesterosCraft
exteriors/gradients, the Minecraft Wiki roof and architectural-terms tutorials, Wikipedia: Timber framing, Jettying, Chimney).
Original design; no build was traced.

## Three references (used for learning technique, not copied)
| What | Real-world fact used | Where it shows |
|---|---|---|
| Massing | a market-town house: stone ground floor, upper floor jettied over the street, steep gable to the street, a lower service wing | hall x6..14, oven house west, flour shed east, jetty on the north gable |
| Style | half-timbering: a timber frame with non-structural plaster infill on a stone plinth; primary posts, lighter secondary members | dark oak log posts at corners/bay ends, planks for braces, plaster panels, stone ramp below |
| Detail | one grouped stack serves all fireplaces; brick shaft, corbelled cap, 2 above the roof | the 2 x 2 oven stack at x4..5, z5..6 against the hall's west wall |

## Ramps (light to dark, base first)
| Role | Blocks | Placement |
|---|---|---|
| plinth | cobblestone, mossy cobblestone, andesite | y0 ring; moss near the ground |
| stone | stone bricks, cracked, mossy, andesite | ground storey y1..4, pilasters, oven stack base |
| plaster | white terracotta, calcite, light grey terracotta | upper floor y5..7 and gables, grey low (under the jetty) |
| timber | dark oak log, stripped dark oak log, dark oak planks | posts / beams / braces: frame, bressumer, plate, door and hatch frames |
| roof | spruce stairs (field), dark oak stairs (eave, rake, patches), mud brick stairs (patches) | 45 degrees, dark oak eave and rake |
| brick | bricks, red nether bricks, mud bricks | the stack shaft (exact quotas, clustered) |
| floor | spruce, oak, dark oak planks | interior floors |
| accents | waxed cut copper, copper trapdoor, copper bars / grate | hatch lintel and awning, oven bars, ridge louvre |

## Massing and silhouette
Hall 9 x 9, plate y8, eave y8, ridge y12 + slab cap y13; oven stack top y14 + crown y15 (the only thing above the ridge);
oven house roof ridge y5 (cross gable, west gable end); flour shed lean-to y3..4. Greyscale test: roof darkest band, plaster
lightest, stone mid; the sign board and the jetty bressumer read as horizontal bands.
