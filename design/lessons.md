# Lessons (Pixel Pat)

One dated bullet per lesson: what worked, what looked wrong, how it was fixed.

## Technique notes (in my own words)
- Hue shifting: each ramp step towards the light moves the hue towards yellow, each step towards shadow moves it towards red/purple. Food stays warm and shadows don't turn muddy grey.
- Pillow shading means shading that follows the outline evenly. Avoid it by picking the light direction first and letting the shadow build up on the far side only.
- Round objects read as round when the shading bands are crescents shifted away from the light, not concentric rings.
- At 64 px, 4–5 value steps per material are enough; more steps give banding (stair-step stripes of near-equal colours).
- Clusters: group pixels of one colour into blobs of 3+ pixels. Lone pixels read as noise when the texture is shown small.

## Job log
- 2026-10-18 (cookie): Shading from a height field (rounded rim + dome + bumps) lit by one normal·light dot product, then quantised into a ramp, gives consistent top-left light for free. Repeatable for any round object.
- 2026-10-18 (cookie): Value noise as surface bumps made diagonal streaks that looked like wood grain. Gaussian "lumps" (round mounds) read as baked dough instead. Keep the lump amplitude low (0.5–0.9 against a 6 px rim), or overlapping back-sides form big dark smears that look like stains.
- 2026-10-18 (cookie): Chunks shaded with a 1 px edge rule looked flat. Giving each chunk its own small height field (distance transform → dome) and lighting it like the dough made them look like real 3D chocolate.
- 2026-10-18 (cookie): 1 px crack lines looked like scratches at 64 px, even with a lit lip. Dropped them; a clean face with soft lumps reads better. Cracks need 2+ px width or shouldn't be there at all.
- 2026-10-18 (cookie): The orphan cleanup on the shade map alone isn't enough: contact shadows added later make new lone pixels. Run a final cleanup on the finished image (no same colour among 8 neighbours → majority of the 4 neighbours), keeping diagonal 1 px lines and chocolate glints.
- 2026-10-18 (cookie): Checking 1× next to ×4 on light and dark backgrounds caught nothing this time, but it confirmed that the dark outline keeps the silhouette readable on dark backgrounds. Keep doing it.
- 2026-10-18 (cookie 3D): Fitting the slab stack by "maximum covered area with few holes" gave a 1-unit-tall centre slab with nubs sticking out. Minimising a cost (uncovered painted pixels + 3 × covered transparent pixels) over all boundary choices gave a round, symmetric stack; also forbid a top slab narrower than ~3.5 units or it becomes a nub. 3 iterations.
- 2026-10-18 (cookie 3D): The trimmed art lost its dark outline on the slab edges and looked sliced. A derived face texture (art masked to the slab silhouette + outline redrawn on its edge) fixed it; the source art stays untouched.
- 2026-10-18 (cookie 3D): The first edge texture (profile bands shifted per run) read as wood grain. Fixed lips at the faces, a flat golden middle and irregular light blister clusters read as baked crust.
- 2026-10-18 (icons): Height-field shading on flat rectangles (a head) makes a wide dark rim that reads as a wooden box. For boxy parts paint a flat base plus one lit and one shadow column by hand; keep height-field shading for round parts (hat puffs, grain ears).
- 2026-10-18 (icons): Thin rotated ellipses (grain ears 1.75 px wide) shattered into noise once outlined, and the sheaf looked like a torch. Ears 2+ px wide, each with its own outline ring painted before its fill (back to front), stay separate and readable. 1 px stalks/awns don't survive at 32 px.
- 2026-10-18 (icons): ×8 previews of a 32 px icon are too small to judge in the image viewer; use ×16 for icons, plus the 1×/2×/4× strip on light and dark.
- 2026-10-18 (tools): The worktree sandbox refuses Bash commands that use shell variables or loops, or pipe text mentioning the VCS tool into python. Use literal paths, the Edit tool, or a scratch Python script.
- 2026-10-19 (house-default): A 45-degree stair roof on 3-high walls puts the ridge at y6, so a chimney that clears the ridge pushes the smoke point out of a 7-high box. Alternating stairs and bottom slabs (half-block steps) gave a shallow cottage roof with the ridge at y5 and room for the chimney top plus its smoke cell. Pick the roof pitch from the height budget first.
- 2026-10-19 (house-default): Front eaves at wall-top height (y3 over walls y1..y3) leave the windows (y2) and the door clear; eaves one lower would cover the windows and block the doorway.
- 2026-10-19 (house-default): Hand-written block states drift easily. Generating the grid in code and asserting pane connections, stair shapes, door halves and hanging-block ceilings against the neighbours caught nothing wrong this time but makes later edits safe. Iso renders with a thin dark edge per face (lib/voxel_render.py) read well for judging proportions; 2 iterations (added potted tulips by the step).
- 2026-10-20 (factory): Building the grid from block data strings (not chars) and letting the generator compute pane/bars/fence connections from the neighbours, then assigning palette chars automatically, removed the hand-kept state table; the shared validator in `lib/house_lib.py` still checks every connection independently. The cottage refactor onto the lib kept `default.yml` and its previews byte-identical (checked with `cmp`).
- 2026-10-20 (factory): A 1 x 1 stack with four corbel stairs reads as a cross (grave marker) in the front elevation. Filling the corners with top slabs makes a square corbelled cap. Check thin vertical parts in the flat elevation view, not only in 3/4.
- 2026-10-20 (factory): A factory silhouette comes from contrast in height, not size: one tall stack as the only thing above the ridge, a short stack on a low lean-to, a vent on the ridge. Lean-tos grow the footprint sideways and give the facade depth for free. 1 review pass.
- 2026-10-20 (factory layout): "Free cell" checks must be per level: a column-wide check failed the belt cell under the hatch awning. Belt cells need belt level free; standing points need feet and head level free.
- 2026-10-20 (fence): Posts placed by a fixed rhythm land right beside gate pillars; skip posts within 1 cell of a pillar or corner. Keep fence styles to non-directional blocks so one style works on every side.
- 2026-10-21 (conveyor belt): A scrolling texture loops seamlessly when the pattern's period in the scroll direction equals the frame count × step (8 texels, 8 frames, 1 texel each); build one period, tile it, and `np.roll` per frame. No `interpolate`: blending smears hard slat edges.
- 2026-10-21 (conveyor belt): Small 2 × 2 "L" wear clusters on the rubber read as letters/glyphs at ×8. Short horizontal scuffs along the slats read as wear. 2 iterations.
- 2026-10-21 (conveyor belt): Keeping the belt frame to one column inside the belt cells (logs across the belt as rollers, copper grate housings between) avoids clashing with props beside the hatch (a barrel) and makes backup/undo exactly the belt cells. Side rails went into the item model instead.
- 2026-10-21 (camera): Framing by eye from a top view put the first camera too close; projecting the plot's extreme points with the game's FOV and searching positions numerically, then judging 3 rendered candidates, found a 3/4 view that fits a 4:3 screen. A straight-on front view hid the pedestal behind the gate arch. Validate framing on the narrowest screen (4:3), not 16:9. 2 iterations.
- 2026-10-21 (upgrade icons): A hand with one tall finger beside shorter ones reads as a rude gesture; raised fingers must have equal height and the curled ones stay short. A tall arrow overlapping a cookie only reads when the arrow is clearly above it and the chips are 2 px, not plus shapes. 2 iterations.
- 2026-10-21 (upgrade icons): Reuse one parametrised glyph (hand/rolling pin with a ramp) for tiered items; recolouring by ramp gives a consistent family cheaply.
- 2026-10-22 (decor): plot decoration `houses/decor.yml` from `design/scripts/decor_style.py`; keep-out as inclusive rectangles asserted in the generator. Trees with radius-2 canopies hit the fence ring and the factory margin on the first run: centre trees 3 cells from the ring or use radius 1. A top-down plan PNG was enough to judge density; no in-game look yet.
- 2026-10-22 (basket): Nested fill steps (fill k = base + pile layers 1..k) let one atlas per tier serve all five models with identical UVs; a face-dedup shelf packer sized the atlas (golden needed 256). Face UVs come from pixel size at a fixed 2 texels per unit, so density is consistent. Pile discs at radius 3.4 px with dark chips looked like noise; radius 4.2+ and fewer chips fixed it. Brick-offset weave reads as bricks; fine for a placeholder. 2 iterations.
- 2026-10-22 (basket fix): ItemDisplay NONE: model y 8 = display position. The basket was modelled from y 0 and showed invisible/half-buried in game. Check against conveyor_belt before shipping a placed model; the generator now asserts lowest element y == 8. gui/fixed translations were lowered to keep icons framed.
- 2026-10-22 (building-roster): Writing the 6 buildings as local-coordinate (x/z centred, y from floor) box lists and shifting once (+8) in the model writer made the "bottom at model y 8" assert trivial and kept footprints importable by the slot/decor scripts (one source of truth). A shared per-building atlas with a face packer + procedural painters (bricks, planks, crate, stripes, swirl) gave a coherent look fast; renders from front and back at 3 stages caught a floating gold bar (portal trim) in one pass.

Slot zones: check footprints as centred squares so any yaw is safe, and give decor a margin on top; the first slot layout hit the factory zone at z 8 and needed 2 loops. Icon mistake: using `rect(0,0,0,0)` as an "empty mask" painted pixel (0,0); use `if`.
- 2026-10-24 (build-quality, renderer): flat average-colour previews hid every real problem (dark mass, wrong materials). Rendering the real vanilla block models and textures from the client jar (blockstate -> model parent chain -> elements, rotations, tints; `lib/block_models.py` + `lib/tex_render.py`, reads the jar in place, nothing committed) took 1 afternoon and paid back in the first image: it matched the Chunky shots almost 1:1. - 2026-10-24 (build-quality, lint): rules written as asserts caught real mistakes at once (all windows unlit, a ramp that was a single block, a 10-block straight gravel edge, a lone woodpile). Two lint bugs of my own: the inside/outside flood fill leaked through mullion fences and slabs (make only air and small props passable), and random cluster noise left one ramp variant unused in small builds (use exact quotas for small sets: `Ramp.assign`).
- 2026-10-24 (factory rework): shutters on every plaster column hid all the plaster and the facade turned dark brown; plaster must be the biggest area (square panels 3 wide, posts at bay ends, shutters only where plaster stays visible). A stripped-log band right above another log band reads as one heavy beam: make one of them stone.
- 2026-10-24 (factory rework): a lateral stack attached to the hall's side wall (2 x 2, stone base, brick shaft, corbel ring, slab crown) reads as one chimney; the old tall plus-shaped boiler stack read as an industrial tower. A 4 x 4 slab rim around the crown looked like a hat: stairs ring plus two crown slabs is enough. 3 review rounds.
- 2026-10-24 (cottage): a cross-gable valley is the risky part of any roof; a lower wing in line with the hall (ridge parallel, wing abutting the hall's gable wall) gives two volumes and two roof heights with no valley at all.
- 2026-10-24 (decor): mixing four ground blocks per patch (gravel + dirt path + coarse dirt + cobblestone) looked like a checkerboard from the viewpoint, and `dirt_path` is a bright tan next to grass. One base block + one or two variants per patch, one tone per role (lanes gravel, plazas cobble, yards coarse dirt/podzol), and fewer, smaller patches so the lawn stays the main surface. 2 rounds.
