- House designs live in `src/main/resources/houses/<id>.yml` (owned by model-designer since 2026-10-19, `.claude/ownership.json`), generated by `design/scripts/house_<id>.py`; spec `design/house-<id>.md`. Format: size/origin/palette/layers/points/signs (see `design/house-default.md`). Stable while the starter-house contract holds.
- Ownership only covers `design/*.md` and `design/scripts/**` in `design/`, so preview PNGs go to `design/scripts/previews/` (verified in ownership.json, 2026-10-19). Asked Rick for a `design/house-*.png` glob; check whether it was added before moving them.
- `tools/validate_assets.py` does not look at `src/main/resources/houses/`; the generator's own asserts are the check (2026-10-19).
- Block facts used (vanilla, source knowledge, not yet confirmed in game): door `facing=north` puts the plate on the south side of its cell; sign/hanging-sign `rotation=8` = front faces north; flower pots need no support; hanging lantern/sign survive under a bottom-half stair.

**Why:** avoids re-deriving the format and the ownership workaround next time.
**How to apply:** reuse `design/scripts/lib/voxel_render.py` and the validation in `house_default.py` for new designs (e.g. bakery-growth). See [[pack-facts-26-3]].
